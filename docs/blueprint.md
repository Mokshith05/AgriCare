# **App Name**: AgriProtect AI

## Core Features:

- Image Upload & Processing: Allow users to upload crop images for analysis and prepare images for model processing.
- AI-Powered Pest & Disease Detection: Use a pre-trained PyTorch/TensorFlow model to identify crop diseases and pests in uploaded images. Generate the level of the infection/infestation.
- Treatment Recommendations: Based on detected diseases/pests, provide organic treatment recommendations using an AI tool that analyzes Indian government (ICAR, Kisan Suvidha) resources. Multilingual support for treatment descriptions.
- Weather-Based Alerts: Integrate with a weather API to alert farmers of disease-prone conditions for their specific crops.
- Disease/Pest Encyclopedia: A comprehensive encyclopedia with information on various crop diseases and pests.
- Preventive Care Tips: Offer preventive care tips for various crops, based on government agricultural data.
- Field Activity Logging: Enable farmers to log their field activities and track crop health.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Emerald Green (#50C878) to reflect growth, nature, and health.
- Background color: Light Beige (#F5F5DC) providing a natural and calm backdrop.
- Accent color: Earthy Brown (#A0522D) for highlighting calls to action, tying into earth tones.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif that combines a modern look and a little warmth or personality.
- Use nature-inspired icons that reflect agriculture and organic farming. Icons should have a thin outline style for a modern aesthetic.
- A clean, responsive layout with intuitive navigation. Prioritize usability for farmers with varying levels of tech experience.
- Subtle, nature-inspired animations to enhance user engagement without distracting from the app's core functionality.
